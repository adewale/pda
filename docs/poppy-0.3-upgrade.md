# Poppy 0.3: what has to change

**The plan of record is [poppy-0.3-plan.md](poppy-0.3-plan.md):** support the two newest published versions, drop 0.1 now, and take a new version in about two working days, decided 28 October. This page keeps what each 0.3 change touches; where its order of work, gates or cross-cutting notes differ, the plan replaces them. Every open problem in the 0.3 text, with current line numbers, is in [poppy-0.3-issues.md](poppy-0.3-issues.md).

Draft 0.3 of the Personal Agent Protocol appeared on [personalagentprotocol.org](https://personalagentprotocol.org/docs/0.3/spec) between 24 and 25 October 2026, alongside the published, frozen 0.2. This page says what it changes for a Personal Agent, which of our files each change lands in, and the order to do it in. It is a plan, not a record: **nothing of 0.3 is implemented**, and the ledger and evidence sections that [poppy-0.2-upgrade.md](poppy-0.2-upgrade.md) has will be written when it is. The text review, bugs and contradictions in 0.3 itself, is [spec-issues-0.3.md](spec-issues-0.3.md).

## Where things stand

- **0.2 is published and frozen** (24 October). Its text is identical to our 19 October pin ([`spec/draft-0.2-2026-10-19/`](../spec/draft-0.2-2026-10-19/README.md)) apart from the site's links becoming relative; we diffed the spec, guides, Operations and Extensions pages. The 0.2 pin, ledger and `npm run check:spec` stand as they are.
- **0.3 is "in progress"**: its changelog "grows until 0.3 is published", and every 0.x may break the one before. The site serves it at `/docs/0.3/…`, and `/docs/spec` now points there.
- **Upstream's repository has caught up, but 0.3 isn't published.** By 26 October `main` was `a95b757`, 31 commits past our 0.2 pin, with `content/versions/0.3` and an official kit whose schemas and Company validator cover 0.3 (excluded tasks, `author`, `protected_resources`, Token Exchange). So there is now a commit `npm run spec:import` can take. The changelog still says 0.3 is "In progress" and "grows until 0.3 is published".
- **The evidence for 0.3 is three site snapshots**, made with `node scripts/spec-snapshot.mjs --save`: [01:47](../spec/draft-0.3-retrieved-2026-10-25/README.md) and [06:13 UTC](../spec/draft-0.3-retrieved-2026-10-25T0613Z/README.md) on 25 October, and [18:34 UTC on 26 October](../spec/draft-0.3-retrieved-2026-10-26/README.md). Between the first and the last the changelog went from eighteen entries to twenty-nine, the spec from 1,423 lines to 1,561, and section 8 and 4.10 appeared. The table below is against the 26 October text. The pin for an implementation must still be an upstream commit, as [upgrading.md](upgrading.md) requires.
- **Operations is still called v1 but has changed**: its 3.2 and 5.2 now say `insufficient_scope` where 0.2's said `sign_in_required`, and its 4.3 example uses `author` where it used `sender` ([T36](spec-issues-0.3.md#t36)). The Extensions page is unchanged.
- **No live Company declares 0.3** that we know of. The four live 0.2 targets in [live-0.2-testing.md](live-0.2-testing.md) and every peer in [`interop/impls/`](../interop/impls/) speak 0.1 or 0.2.

Cross-cutting, outside any one entry:

- **Version plumbing.** `src/poppy/protocol-version.ts` gains `"0.3"`, its revision and, once upstream has one, its commit; `isDraft2` (11 calls, in 17 files with the other `"0.2"` checks) becomes a profile selection, and Operations behaviour is chosen by the document's `protocol_version`, since its own `version` no longer says ([T36](spec-issues-0.3.md#t36)). Unknown 0.x stays refused. The validator gets a `draft-0.3` profile and `src/validator/levels.ts` its basis mapping; the interop runner, adapters and `known-failures.json` get a `0.3` lane; `interop/impls/pda-mock` registers 0.3 variants of `lenient-company` and `strict-company`, which are the only 0.3 Companies there will be until peers move.
- **The ledger.** `spec/requirements-0.3.json` with a disposition for each of the twenty-nine entries and the guides entry, generated into this page by `npm run spec:docs`, and `scripts/check-spec.mjs` extended to a second pin. The pin is an upstream commit (`a95b757` or later), imported with `npm run spec:import`, so the official kit comes with it; `check:spec` then verifies both pins.
- **The schema.** One additive migration to `poppy_sessions` (Session Key and thumbprint, `session_id` nullable); `test/rows.test.ts` checks the declarations. Rows from 0.1 and 0.2 Sessions keep working; a 0.3 Session is never resumed by an older binary, so [upgrading.md](upgrading.md)'s rollback note gains a line: stop 0.3 interactions before rolling back.
- **Docs.** `pda-spec.md` (the header's version statement and Appendix A, which cites section numbers that moved: section 5 is rewritten, 9.5 and 9.6 are new), `docs/capabilities.md`, `docs/readings.md` (the readings in [spec-issues-0.3.md](spec-issues-0.3.md) under "Our reading"), `spec/grounding.md` (quotes for every 0.3 requirement we claim), `docs/conformance-levels.md` if any basis changes, `docs/interop-spec.md` (a browser-join scenario, a Token Exchange scenario, a key-rotation-is-a-new-Session scenario), and the privacy policy if the Session Key changes what a Company can link (it doesn't: Sessions of one User are already linked by User ID).

## Order of work

The 0.2 upgrade's rule holds: change only the decisions 0.3 changes, reuse everything else, and never let a newer version fall back to an older profile. In this order, each step shippable behind the 0.3 profile with 0.1 and 0.2 untouched:

1. **Pin and profile.** Wait for, or ask upstream for, the 0.3 commit; import it; add the version, the profile skeleton and the ledger with every entry "Not started". Until then, the snapshot is the text and nothing in `src/` changes.
2. **Discovery and validator** (changes 1, 8, 9, 19, 21, 2's reserved prefix). Pure parsing and checks, fully testable against the mock and the snapshot's examples. The validator can ship 0.3 before the client does, as it did for 0.2.
3. **Session Keys and the token endpoint** (4, 12, 13, 17, 23). The large one, and everything after depends on it. Do the mock first, from the spec text, then the client, then the property model. Keep the 4.8 disambiguation ([T1](spec-issues-0.3.md#t1)) and the start-before-sign-in rule ([T2](spec-issues-0.3.md#t2)) as explicit readings.
4. **Scopes** (2, 10, 3, 6). Touches the model's tools and the cards, so it needs the page tests and a run-through in `docs/using.md`.
5. **Browser join** (5, 7's code handling). Mock first, then `browser.ts`; the `__Host-` prefix implies `Secure`, which the Hypothesis lane already satisfies by presenting `https://` URLs to the mock over 127.0.0.1.
6. **MCP Token Exchange** (14), **secrets tests** (7), **sign-out** (18).
7. **Interop lane, docs, Appendix A, changelog entry**, then a staging deploy against our own 0.3 mock. There is no live 0.3 Company to check against yet; say so in the changelog rather than counting the mock as one.

Don't start step 3 while the draft is moving under it unless the owner accepts rework: of the twenty-nine entries, the Session Key design is the one most likely to shift after review, and it is the one the rest hang on.

## What 0.3 does to the 0.1 catalogue

Each of the 64 findings in [spec-issues.md](spec-issues.md) is graded against 0.2 and 0.3 in [spec-issues-0.3.md](spec-issues-0.3.md#the-01-catalogue-in-02-and-03). In 0.2, 16 are resolved, 14 partly and 34 open; in 0.3, 21 resolved, 14 partly, 28 open and one worse (N4, step-up revocation). Four of the five Critical findings are resolved; N10, which hosts may receive a Session Token, is not. Of the thirteen High findings, two are resolved, five partly and six open: N45, N43, N25, N5, S22 and S20. The readings in [readings.md](readings.md) for the open ones still apply to a 0.3 profile.

## Are we ready to implement 0.3?

Not properly, not yet, though two gates opened on 26 October. "Properly" here means what [upgrading.md](upgrading.md) asks of every version: a pinned upstream commit, a ledger entry for each change, the official checks, and evidence against someone else's implementation. On 25 October none of those was possible. By 26 October a commit and the official checks exist, but the text is still changing, the sign-in questions are open, nobody else speaks 0.3, and our own 0.2 isn't finished:
