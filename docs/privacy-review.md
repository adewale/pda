# Privacy policy and the product choices behind it

Source and UX review, 30 October 2026. This is not a provider-contract or deployment-settings audit. The policy is [`src/privacy.ts`](../src/privacy.ts); [decision 39](decisions.md#39-a-requested-photo-upload-does-not-need-a-second-permission-click) records this PR's change.

## What was bad, and what this PR fixes

1. **The compulsory photo card was redundant consent, not better privacy.** “Print this photo at this Company” already authorizes the necessary upload. Asking again trains people to approve routine prompts and makes the actual price-and-terms decision less distinct. Poppy does not require the extra click, or a quote before upload. Selected task photos now upload normally; a different saved photo still needs a disclosure decision. Company terms, recipient/ownership checks and replay protection remain.
2. **The policy promised a confirmation for every proposed account action. That was already false.** Decision 38 removed preliminary API-write cards. The policy and using guide still described them. They now distinguish routine task requests from Company operations requiring exact-terms approval. Website forms keep their local card.
3. **“Not used to train AI models” claimed control we have not established over every provider/account configuration.** The operator can make a commitment about its own use. An own-key account has its own settings and contract. The text now makes that distinction; no vendor setting was changed or verified by editing the policy.
4. **“Your own storage” did not explain operator access.** Separate Durable Objects isolate people from each other, not from the service operator. Messages and photos are not end-to-end encrypted. The policy now says so, and explicitly says model providers receive image previews, not just “conversation.”
5. **The retention paragraph hid important distinctions.** The newest-200 diagnostic log now has separate per-action copies; Workers Logs' 3/7-day retention does not govern those or AI Gateway/provider copies. Deleting a memory note does not delete the conversation it came from. Deleting a local photo does not delete a Company's copy. The policy now says these things and separates retention by data type.
6. **“Nobody else” was an unhelpful absolute.** The named services and Companies have their own processing and retention. Removing that slogan is more honest than implying that our application controls every downstream copy. Mediated Sign-In is also described accurately: a sign-in form can be in this app, with credentials sent only to the Company's token endpoint, never the model.

## Still bad: product work, not fixed by changing the words

| Problem | Why it is a bad experience | Recommended change |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **“Email the operator; deletion within 30 days.”** | A personal agent holds an unusually intimate record, yet its owner cannot delete it or export it from the page. Calling this a private assistant while basic data control needs a favor from its operator is embarrassing. | Build authenticated self-service export and deletion, with explicit handling of in-flight actions, account revocation and provider/Company copies. Do not pretend that deleting local state cancels an order. Until then, keep the manual commitment visible and maintain a workable deletion procedure. |
| **“Forget” requires a Settings detour for User-supplied notes.** | The distinction protects notes from model/Company deletion, but “forget this” is a normal request to a personal assistant. Refusing it and sending the person hunting in Settings makes the safety mechanism their problem. | Offer a direct, User-operated deletion control in chat for the exact note. Keep the provenance protection; do not simply give a model blanket deletion authority. [`memory-store.ts`](../src/tools/memory-store.ts), [`memory.tsx`](../src/ui/memory.tsx). |
| **A deleted note remains in the indefinite conversation.** | “Forget everything” sounds broader than deleting the memory table. The agent can still read the original and infer it again. A warning makes this honest, not good. | Distinguish “delete saved notes” from deleting conversation data. Add a real conversation deletion/reset control and explain what Company records survive. [`history.ts`](../src/history.ts), [`using.md`](using.md#what-to-ask). |
| **Photos vanish after 30 days without a useful retention control.** | A short default is defensible; silently losing the photo someone expects to reprint is not. The authenticated delete API exists, but sent photos need an obvious deletion control, not an API exercise. | Show expiry beside photos, offer deletion of a sent photo, and warn before it becomes unavailable. Any optional longer retention needs an explicit product/policy decision, not silent indefinite storage. [`attachments.ts`](../src/attachments.ts), [`ui/attachments.tsx`](../src/ui/attachments.tsx). |
| **Diagnostic copies have no time expiry.** | Count/byte bounds limit storage, but an inactive person's action records can remain indefinitely. These are safer than message logs, not devoid of personal information: Company domains, timing and activity still matter. | Decide a time limit for diagnostic copies, separately from the durable action ledger needed to prevent replay. Do not prune execution identities just to shorten log retention. [`flow-diagnostics.ts`](../src/flow-diagnostics.ts). |
| **Signing out is not becoming anonymous.** | Poppy's persistent per-Company User ID still links signed-out visits. The policy discloses this, but a person is unlikely to discover it there when pressing Sign out. | Explain the distinction beside sign-out. An anonymous mode would be separate work; don't fake one by breaking existing Session/account bindings. [`client.ts`](../src/poppy/client.ts), [`companies.tsx`](../src/ui/companies.tsx). |

## The unresolved operational check

**AI Gateway logging is still unconfirmed in [the deployment checklist](deployment-tasks.md#1-configuration-and-accounts).** Our safe application logger does not prove that gateway request/response bodies are not retained. The existing Wrangler login cannot read those settings. The operator should verify body logging, retention and provider data-use settings in the dashboard and record the result before making stronger claims. This review did not inspect those settings and found no evidence that training occurred.

## What should not be weakened

Keep exact Company-terms approval, task-bound recipients and file selection, credential isolation, account/Session withdrawal, owner-only attachment access, conservative unknown-outcome holds and logs without bodies or signed URLs. Those protect real boundaries. Removing a redundant upload click is not a reason to remove them.
